Developed a statistical framework for jointly modeling multivariate longitudinal biomarkers and interval-censored event times, addressing the challenge that disease onset timing is often only known to fall within an interval due to discrete clinical visit schedules. The model captures biomarker trajectories that change at an unobserved disease onset time, simultaneously estimating how biomarkers predict onset and how trajectories shift after initiation. Validated through simulation studies and applied to the PREDICT-HD cohort to investigate interactions between cognitive and motor dysfunction during Huntington's Disease progression.
