Investigated the prognostic significance of cognitive and other geriatric impairments identified via geriatric assessment in older adults with AML. Used K-M survival curves, log-rank tests, and multivariable Cox regression (adjusting for WBC count and AML risk categories) to identify three types of impairments and number of impairments that significantly affect overall survival.
